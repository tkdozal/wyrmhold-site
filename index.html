The game

The hold is the run

Wyrmhold is a fortress grown out of a crag, and a dragon sits at the top of it. Every run is a road up through its holdings: the crypt beneath it, the cemetery at its walls, and at last the castle itself. You walk the rooms in first person and choose which doors to open. What waits behind them is decided by cards.

Cards are the fight

Combat is turn-based and card-driven, and every card you play is a blow your hero actually throws. A slash is a slash, a shield raised is a shield raised, a fireball leaves the staff and lands.

Nothing hits you unseen

Enemies telegraph their intent a turn ahead; you answer with the hand you drew. Block, Bleed, Burn, Strength, Weak and Vulnerable decide who is standing when the dust settles.

Die, draft, climb again

Every fight won offers a card to your deck. Every level cleared makes the next one harder and carries half of what you built into it. Chests, potions and trinkets along the way.

Four heroes, four decks

Choose who climbs

The Warrior

Sword & shield

Absorbs punishment and gives it back. The only hero who heals between fights.

The Elf

Daggers & longbow

Takes an extra action every turn and has far too little health to waste one. Throwing knives, feints that make the enemy miss, and a longbow for the finish.

The Valkyrie

Spear & shield

Begins every fight already braced, and profits from being swung at. A parry that stops a blow whole and answers it, and wounds that bleed.

The Wizard

Staff & fire

Draws a sixth card every turn and hits hardest per card. Fire that burns, illusions that miss, and a spell that melts an enemy's armour into damage.

The climb

Two roads, one wyrm
