Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the website wyrmholdthegame.com and the video game Wyrmhold (together, "Wyrmhold"). It is published by DHG Game Studio, a trade name of Tim Dozal, an individual ("we", "us"). It explains what data is captured, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how long it is kept.

The short version

  • Wyrmhold is a single-player game. We run no servers and receive no personal data from it.
  • The store version you play (Steam or the Epic Games Store) uses that store's account services for sign-in, achievements, progress stats, your online status shown to friends, and cloud saves. That data is held by the store, not by us.
  • The website uses no cookies, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

This website

We do not ask you to enter any personal information on this website, and it sets no cookies. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), may process standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not receive or use that data to identify you. The site loads its own fonts and images from the same server and does not call third-party services.

Links to Steam, the Epic Games Store, social networks or other sites take you away from this website. Those services have their own privacy policies and we do not control them.

The game on your computer

Wyrmhold saves your progress, unlocks and settings as files on your own computer. The game does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers, and it sends no gameplay or device data to us.

Store versions: Epic Games Store and Steam

What is captured

Epic Games Store version. When the game starts, it signs you in through Epic Account Services with your Epic Games account. With your permission, given on Epic's sign-in screen, the game receives:

  • your basic profile: your Epic account ID, display name, preferred language and linked account display names;
  • permission to set your online presence (your status as your Epic friends see it).

While you play, the game sends the following to Epic Online Services, tied to your account:

  • the achievements you unlock;
  • three progress counts: breakables smashed, potions drunk and artifacts found;
  • a short status line, for example "In the main menu" or "The Warrior (Lv 4) – Forest".

If you turn on the store's cloud saves, your save files are copied to the store.

Steam version. The game uses your Steam account through Steamworks to record the same achievements, progress counts and status line, and Steam Cloud keeps a copy of your save files.

How it is used

Only to provide the features above:

  • signing you in;
  • recording and showing your achievements and progress;
  • showing your status to your friends;
  • syncing your saves between your computers.

It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and the game does not read your friends list.

How it is shared

We do not receive, sell or share this data with anyone. It goes only to the store you play on:

  • Epic Games, Inc. for the Epic version, processed under the Epic Games Privacy Policy;
  • Valve Corporation for the Steam version, processed under the Steam Privacy Policy.

Your achievements, progress and status are visible to other players only as each store's own privacy settings allow. From the stores we receive only aggregated sales and usage reports that do not identify you.

How long it is kept

  • Online status: shown only while you are playing, and cleared when you quit.
  • Achievements and progress counts: kept by the store as part of your account until you or the store delete them.
  • Save files: stay on your computer, and in the store's cloud saves if you use them, until you delete them.
  • Us: we keep none of this data, because none of it reaches us.

Your choices

  • Declining sign-in. You can decline Epic's sign-in permission. The game still plays, without achievements, progress stats or online status.
  • Revoking access. You can remove Wyrmhold's access to your Epic account at any time from your Epic Games account settings (Apps and Accounts).
  • Steam settings. Steam lets you control your profile and game details privacy in its settings.
  • Deletion. To delete data a store holds, use that store's account tools or contact the store. You can also write to us and we will help.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We keep them only as long as needed to deal with your request, and then delete them.

Children

Wyrmhold is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. We hold none from the game; for data the stores hold, see "Your choices" above, or contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Wyrmhold handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
